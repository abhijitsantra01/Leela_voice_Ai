Your space to speak.

Press “Let's talk”, allow your microphone, and speak naturally. End the session to release the microphone. No wake word is needed.

Live audio and typed messages are sent to Google Gemini. Notes are kept in this tab unless you download them. Your API key stays on the local server.

Translation listens and interprets into your chosen language. Transcription makes live notes without speaking back. Those models do not use the five companion voices.

Model access and quota depend on your Gemini account. The sample button plays your supplied recording, not a preview of the selected voice. AI can make mistakes.
